# Retired policy proposal checker

The POL-02 comparison is complete. Its standalone proposal checker and launcher
have been removed from the active checkout. The checker used to confirm that a
proposed study, selected files and reviewed documents agreed before funding or
key entry. It did not spend money or authorize execution.

The [retirement guide](POLICY-LAUNCH.md) lists the removed interfaces and their
pinned historical sources. The [original checker guide](https://github.com/wavemanco/buddha/blob/e86bac23d174263245360c5a388924f362012f0b/docs/POLICY-HANDOFF.md)
and [checker source](https://github.com/wavemanco/buddha/blob/e86bac23d174263245360c5a388924f362012f0b/buddha/policy_handoff.py)
remain available in Git history.

The [study protocol](POLICY-COMPARISON.md), [configuration evidence](conformance/pol02-handoff-config-delivery.json)
and [measured results](POLICY-COMPARISON-RESULTS.md) retain their original meaning.
Frozen executing copies, authorizations and replies remain unchanged. The shared
policy controller and passive readers are retained for evidence and fixtures.

[PLAN.md](PLAN.md) owns current work. This retirement introduces no replacement
command and no new policy run; the old allocation is closed.
