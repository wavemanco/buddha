# WildChat preparation for the next improver comparison

October 25, 2026. The user selected and reconfirmed official WildChat for the
next sealed IMP-01 comparison. [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) remains the roadmap. This
preparation creates data and custody records, not a funded experiment or a
launch command. No generation or judging calls have started.

## What is prepared

The complete official WildChat source contains 1,000 tasks, with ten answers
required for each assessed task. Original text and IDs are preserved. The
existing task loader validates the source under a WildChat release configuration;
the official evaluator commands, models and settings are unchanged.
Upstream still points to `64a23be6b18ec1dacc298830aab276061b6e143c` at this check.

| Item | Prepared population |

Here, a family means related input prompts. During a live evaluation,
NoveltyBench separately groups each task's ten generated answers for scoring.

The [opaque population map](conformance/imp01-wildchat-population.json) records
every task's hashes, family, type code, phase and uncertainty flags. It contains
no prompt text or task-specific descriptions. The map itself makes no eligibility
attestation; the later separate custody declaration supplies the scoped final
eligibility. [The preparation record](conformance/imp01-wildchat-preparation.json)
binds both artifacts and their evidence.

## How the information stays separated

A fresh data-custody context and its custody-only delegates read all 1,000
primary prompts in full. They received no candidate sources, generated answers,
scores or optimization history. Descriptive notes stayed within that role.
Only IDs, hashes, opaque group/type codes, generic taxonomy, counts, coverage
and limits returned to the optimization host.

Root/source-author and host optimization decisions remain part of the named
optimization process. They are not excluded to make a custody check pass.
The exact P/C sources were frozen before this preparation. Trusted programs may
read complete source bytes for validation and hash private evidence; they do
not display task text or descriptive notes to the optimization model. These
custody contexts must not later be reused to author or tune agents in this
experiment.

The declaration records all 1,000 full-prompt disclosures to the actual separate
data-preparation role. It binds 60 evidence files and declares only the exact
26 final members eligible for this process. This is logical role separation,
not encryption or enforced filesystem isolation. Public model-training exposure
and unrecorded outside access remain unknown. Any newly discovered semantic
disclosure into optimization requires stopping and reassessing eligibility.

The improver receives development tasks and complete development feedback.
During final assessment, the generator and judges receive their current task
as usual. Final results do not feed another improvement iteration in that study.

## How tasks were assigned

The original allocation recipe was recorded before root received the family map.
A further custody-only comparison checked possible relationships with the
previously reviewed Curated tasks: 307 planned pairs and 14 additional manual
comparisons. It found no exact matches, one semantic link and nine uncertain
links. Their conservative closure covers eight WildChat tasks. Curated partner
IDs and descriptions stay private because they could reveal WildChat content.

None of the original 26 proposed final tasks was flagged. Two newly linked
components did cross development and validation, however. Before any experiment,
the [updated recipe](conformance/imp01-wildchat-allocation-recipe.json) therefore
joins those whole families and excludes the overlap closure from final selection.
It preserves the earlier preparation rather than overwriting it. Seed 7 and
opaque membership determine the new assignment; no answer quality or benchmark
outcome was available. This conservative exclusion does not claim that a related
task necessarily leaked into agent development.

Whole families first enter the existing deterministic splitter within broad
task-type strata. From its final pools, proportional quotas and a deterministic
whole-family subset rule select exactly 26 tasks; remaining pool families join
validation. The three development tasks come from distinct development families.
Preparation refuses an infeasible count rather than splitting a family or
silently shrinking the panel. The original task-type/family-review uncertainty
flags do not filter selection; the conservative cross-source overlap exclusions
apply separately before final quotas are calculated.

The small final panel includes five broad task types. The other two types are
absent; the three development tasks cover three types. These panels do not
represent every WildChat task type or the entire leaderboard population.
Both official subsets remain necessary for an eventual leaderboard submission.

## Checks and limits

An independent implementation reproduces the split, development selection and
population metadata byte-for-byte, including after family-order and label changes.
The existing source and split validators pass. Saved custody validation passes;
seven modified-record checks correctly reject unknown/exposed final members,
optimizer disclosure, missing tasks, changed hashes, families crossing phases
and selecting an overlap-blocked member that remains unknown.
The guard checks the declaration's consistency; it cannot prove that every actor
obeyed the role boundary. No guard was waived. The original captured runs remain
unchanged.

The family review retains 107 uncertain items across the full source. It examined
234 candidate pairs, including 152 cross-batch pairs with a second opinion;
it did not independently compare every possible pair. Family judgments are
fallible and do not establish independent statistical blocks or human truth.
It covers the official primary prompt field; other raw annotations were not
semantically reviewed.
The custodian's NFKC normalization is a family-review diagnostic; original task
text and the existing native whitespace/casefold validator remain unchanged.
An initial reader mismatch about that diagnostic was retained and corrected
before any allocation was written.

The proposed uncertainty formula gives approximate radii of 6.297594 utility
and 5.667835 distinct answers for this panel under its stated assumptions.
Those are wide margins: modest differences can remain inconclusive. This is
a formula check, not a power analysis, an adopted statistical plan or evidence
that C improves on P.
