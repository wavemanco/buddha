# Retired standalone source launcher

The first and current-source experiments completed under their own allocations.
Their standalone launcher, proposal checker and entry script have been removed
from the active checkout. The current inherited improvement loop uses its own
controller; no replacement launcher or new run is introduced here.

[First results](SOURCE-FIRST-LIVE-RESULTS.md) and [current-source results](SOURCE-CURRENT-LIVE-RESULTS.md)
retain the measured outcomes and their limits. Original frozen executing copies,
approvals, grants, replies and publications remain unchanged. Old commands in
the frozen handoff guides refer to those original copies and do not authorize
new work or spending.

The [passive readiness reader](../buddha/source_launch_state.py) remains available,
along with source search, verified copies, mutation checks and complete feedback.
Retiring the launcher does not remove these current controller safeguards.

Historical interfaces remain available in Git:

- [Entry script](https://github.com/wavemanco/buddha/blob/0e96fdbb3283f3747375597dc0fc1ce78a136a59/docs/conformance/src02-run.py)

cannot fund a new comparison.
